What's in a packet

Eight documents · Every title

01 · Specimen record

The catalog entry. Title, author, year, discipline codes, and a plot summary, on one page.

02 · Teacher introduction

What the packet contains and how to run it, including anything to know before the class meets the book.

03 · Reader introduction

The one page a student reads first, explaining what they are being asked to do and why.

04 · Sources

The research the packet is built on, in MLA style, most of it free to read.

05 · Reading companion

Four sections, roughly a thousand words each: the author, the moment the book was written into, what the story is really about, and the science. The science carries two audits, The Lawbreaker for where the book breaks the rules and The Long Fuse for what it set in motion in the real world.

06 · Quiz, three variants

The multiple choice differs across A, B, and C so answers cannot be traded. The short answers stay the same, so one rubric covers the class.

07 · Answer guide

Keys for all three variants, plus model responses and grading notes for the short answers.

08 · Discussion guide

A 45-minute session, built to be run by a teacher who has not read the book. Every question carries facilitation notes and a text anchor.

Every title in the Core Library gets the same eight, whatever the book. Three are finished and free to download. The rest are in the lab.
