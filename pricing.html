Pricing

What it costs

A packet is a one-time purchase, priced by the length of the book it goes with. No subscription, no account, nothing to log into. You buy it, you download it, it is yours.

Per packet

One-time
Short / novellaunder 150 pages$3
Mid novel150 to 500 pages$5
Long novel500 pages and up$7

Length is the only variable. Every packet holds the same eight documents whatever the book: the specimen record, both introductions, the reading companion, the sources, three quiz variants, the answer guide, and the discussion guide. A longer book gets a longer quiz, and that is where the difference goes.

No subscriptions. Ever.

You are not renting access to your own materials. A packet you downloaded in September still works in May, still works the year after that, and still works if this site is gone. It is a PDF in a folder you control.

Still being worked out Bundles by thread, by discipline, and by author, each at a discount off the sum of its parts. School and district licensing. Both are coming and neither has a number yet. When they do, the numbers will be on this page.

The book itself

The packet is the part that costs money. The book does not have to. Every title on this site leads with borrowing: your public library first, then Libby, which puts library ebooks and audiobooks on a phone with a card you already have. Public domain titles link to Project Gutenberg or Open Library, where they are free to anyone, forever. Anything you would rather own links to Bookshop.org, which sends the money to independent bookstores.

There are no Amazon links anywhere on this site, and there never will be.
